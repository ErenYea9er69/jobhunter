Data Source Architecture & Attribution

Tier 1 • Clean & Easy

Public ATS Career Boards

Providers: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable.

Method: Public JSON job-board endpoints published by vendors for programmatic recruitment usage. No logins, no bot mitigation, 100% compliant direct employer applications.

Tier 1 • Attributed Feeds

Free Remote Feeds & Aggregators

Providers: Remotive, RemoteOK, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, Hacker News Monthly Hiring.

Attribution Compliance: As requested by RemoteOK and Remotive, every job card displays explicit source badges and direct backlinks to the original listing where you apply.

Tier 2 • Major Job Board

Indeed (always attempted)

Status: Included in every search. JobSpy is used when installed; otherwise the built-in public-results fallback is used. Scraping Indeed carries terms-of-service risk.

Use Adzuna (free API key) as an additional way to cover local and non-tech jobs.

Major Job Board • Always attempted

LinkedIn guest listings

Status: Included in every search without credentials.

Risk: LinkedIn's terms restrict automated access and the page layout changes often, so results may be incomplete or blocked. Use at your own risk.
