# ⚡ JobHunter AI Autofill & Copilot Chrome Extension (Jobright Mode)

This companion Chrome/Edge extension runs directly in your browser on Applicant Tracking Systems (**Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, BambooHR, SmartRecruiters, Taleo, iCIMS, Workable**) and connects to your local JobHunter backend (`http://localhost:8080`).

---

## 🚀 Quick Setup (Takes 10 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Navigate to:
   - Chrome: `chrome://extensions`
   - Edge: `edge://extensions`
3. In the top-right corner, toggle on **"Developer mode"**.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left toolbar.
5. Select this exact folder:
   `c:\Users\hp\Desktop\jobhunter\extension`

---

## 🎯 Features

- **1-Click Autofill**: Automatically detects form fields across all major ATS platforms and populates your profile with React 16+ virtual DOM synchronization.
- **Jobright-Style Companion**: Injects the floating in-page copilot (matching Jobright's Cohere design) on job application pages.
- **In-Page Screening Assistant**: Automatically adds a `⚡ Solve with AI` helper above custom screening questions to generate grounded STAR answers.
- **100% Submission Verification**: Automatically detects application success screens ("Application submitted", "Thank you for applying") and registers a confirmed application in JobHunter!
- **100% Local & Private**: Direct communication with your local `http://localhost:8080` engine. Zero external cloud dependencies.
