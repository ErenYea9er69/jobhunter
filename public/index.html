Sources and privacy

Where jobs come from, and where your data stays.

Your data stays on this computer

Your resume, applications, and tailored documents live in data/jobhunter.db. The app sends none of it to tracking services, training sets, or data brokers.

AI Engine & Guardrails

Power tailoring and strategic role analysis with Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, or the built-in local engine.

Deterministic (Active)
Zero-Hallucination Guardrail Active

All AI rewrites are filtered through our local Grounding Guard. If any AI invents a metric, technology, or title you don't possess, it is automatically rejected.

Where jobs come from

Company career pages
Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable. Public endpoints, no login. You apply directly with the employer.
Remote job feeds
Remotive, RemoteOK, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, We Work Remotely, Hacker News. Every job links back to the original post, as these feeds ask.
Indeed
Tried on every search. Indeed restricts scraping in its terms, so results can be partial.
LinkedIn
Public guest listings, tried on every search. LinkedIn restricts automated access and changes its pages often, so results can be partial. Use at your own risk.
Adzuna
Covers local and non-tech jobs. Turns on when you set a free ADZUNA_APP_ID and ADZUNA_APP_KEY.
