How it works

From one file to a full application pipeline in four steps.

1

Upload

Add a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown file, or paste raw text. Review the parsed profile and fix any field before you search.

2

Search

One search covers company career portals, remote feeds, Indeed, and LinkedIn. Duplicate listings merge into one.

3

Tailor

Press Tailor resume on a job. Five tabs hold the resume, ATS score, skill gaps, cover letter, and interview prep for that job.

4

Track

Move each application through five stages, set follow-up dates, and keep interview notes in one place.

Features

Everything between your resume and the offer.

A 0 to 100 match score, with the reasons.

JobHunter checks exact skill overlap, title alignment, work mode (remote or hybrid), and experience seniority. Each job gets a color-coded score badge and a plain-language fit note. The note names the skills you match and the requirements you lack.

A resume refiner that never invents experience.

Press Tailor resume on any job. JobHunter rephrases your summary and bullet points to echo the posting's language, and moves the most relevant skills to the top. It never adds fake companies, job titles, or credentials.

A side-by-side diff shows every change. Accept or reject each one, or edit inline. Your master resume stays untouched, and each job keeps its own saved version.

Resume parser

Reads PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown, or pasted text. It extracts your title, years of experience, skill matrix, work history, and education. You review every field before searching.

Cover letter generator

Writes a concise three-paragraph letter in your tone, using only verified resume facts. Choose Direct & Impactful, Conversational, or Systems-Focused.

ATS-compliant export

Download an ATS Clean PDF or a Word document (.doc). Copy plain text for application forms.

Company watchlist

Track companies such as Stripe, GitLab, Figma, and Shopify. JobHunter watches their public career endpoints and flags matching roles.

Daily digest simulator

Preview a morning briefing email that lists roles above your chosen score threshold, for example 75 percent.

Interview prep studio

Role-specific behavioral questions using the STAR method, technical deep dives, and questions to ask the interviewer.

Privacy

Your resume stays on your computer.

JobHunter stores your master resume in a local database. It tracks no usage data, and one button removes everything.

  • Your master resume lives in a local SQLite file, data/jobhunter.db.
  • Zero data tracking.
  • The interface uses system fonts and makes no outside requests.
  • The Permanently Wipe Data button removes your data instantly.
